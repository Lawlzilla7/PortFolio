LEIDERS VAN LUSMURGIË

This project was developed in collaboration with Epic-We and is based on the values of the European Union. The game we developed is a social game that focuses on debate.

Research:

My goal as a designer within this project was to conduct research into a specific question, using a chosen board game — in my case, Cards Against Humanity. I then used the collected data to arrive at a usable design. The central question I investigated was: ‘Does the context of the game influence the social experience?’

My research consisted of observations during play sessions with two different groups. Group 1 deviated from the standard rules of the game and often chose milder cards... Group 2 played the game as intended: they deliberately chose shocking or provocative cards.

The Results:

With context, the cards were generally perceived as more offensive. At the same time, participants also found the cards with context funnier, although this varied greatly. With context, the cards were also rated as less socially acceptable.

The Process:

A major part of this project involved researching the target audiences and aligning their needs with both user requirements and client expectations. We continuously tested with representative users to validate our decisions.

Iteration:

We began by designing the political party cards. These went through several iterations before reaching the final version. One key improvement was expanding each party’s viewpoints from two to four.
